What CampusOne includes

Four tools built for MIU

Each feature solves a real campus problem that students and staff face every day.

Campus map

Find any building, room or facility on the MIU campus. Search by name or browse interactive floor plans.

Emergency map

Locate the nearest emergency exit, first-aid station, fire extinguisher and AED on campus. Works offline.

Maintenance reports

Report a broken light or faulty equipment. Track the status of your report until it is resolved.

Lost & Found

Post a lost item or browse found items. AI matching suggests likely connections between lost and found reports.

Getting started

Three steps to use CampusOne

  1. 1

    Log in

    Use your MIU university email to log in. Staff accounts are provided by the admin.

  2. 2

    Find or report

    Search the campus map, submit a maintenance request or post a lost or found item.

  3. 3

    Get updates

    Receive notifications when your report changes status or a match is found for your lost item.
